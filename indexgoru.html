    Questions, answered

    How fast is a cow?

    A cow walks about 4 km/h, so a 4 km trip takes around an hour. A horse at a trot covers about 12 km/h. Choose the animal that suits your schedule.

    Can I bring luggage?

    Gorur XL carries luggage and market shopping. Gorur Go and Ghora Express carry one small bag.

    What happens when it rains?

    Ghora Gari and Gorur XL come with rain covers. In heavy storms riders may pause trips, and you won't be charged for a cancelled ride.

    When does gorurgari launch?

    We're getting ready to launch. This site is a preview, so ride requests don't go anywhere yet.
